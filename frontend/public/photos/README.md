# Sample photos

Cells cut from six AI-generated contact sheets the design lead sent on
2026-09-26 (1536x1024 each; sheets 1-3 and 5 are 5x4, sheets 4 and 6 are 5x5).
A file name is its position: `s2r3c1.jpg` = sheet 2, row 3, column 1.
Each cell is about 295x245 (sheet 4: 295x197), JPEG quality 82.

Only youth scenes from sheets 1-4 are used; sheets 5-6 (families) are off-theme.
